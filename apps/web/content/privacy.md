
# Privacy Policy

*Last updated: 15 November 2026*

Cheddar is a play-money prediction market built for the Hack Club community. This page explains what we collect and why.

## What we collect

When you sign in with Hack Club Auth we receive:

- your name and email address
- your Slack ID and verification status

When you use Cheddar we also store:

- your Hackatime coding hours (used to award Latta Cheese)
- your trades, positions, proposals and reports
- your profile settings and referral activity
- session cookies that keep you logged in

## How we use it

- To log you in and keep you signed in
- To award Latta Cheese for your coding time
- To run markets, show leaderboards and prevent cheating
- To moderate the site and keep an audit log of admin actions

## What's public

Your display name, profile, trades and leaderboard position may be visible to other users. Your email address is never shown publicly.

## Who we share it with

We don't sell your data. We use service providers to run the site: Vercel, Neon, and Vercel Analytics for anonymous usage stats. We also read data from Hack Club Auth and Hackatime.

## Cookies

We use a session cookie to keep you logged in and a small preference for light/dark theme. We don't use advertising cookies.

## Your choices

You can ask us to delete your account and data at any time by contacting [@404Navdeep](https://hackclub.enterprise.slack.com/team/U083T3ZP6AV) on Hack Club Slack or navdeep@lives-on.tech. Some records, such as settled trades and the audit log, may be kept in anonymised form so markets stay fair.

## Children

Cheddar is intended for teenagers verfified in the Hack Club community.

## Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows when it last changed.
